Last updated: August 21, 2026

Jason Stoops ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Jason's Game mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Jason's Game does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).**

The App does not create accounts, does not store any data locally or remotely, and does not track scores, progress, or settings between sessions.

## 2. Third-Party Services

The App **does not use any third-party services** (such as analytics, advertising, or crash reporting) that collect data from your device.

## 3. Permissions

The App requires no special permissions (such as access to your location, camera, or contacts) and does not require internet access to function.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
